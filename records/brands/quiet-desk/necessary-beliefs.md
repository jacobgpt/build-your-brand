# Quiet Desk — Necessary Beliefs

The things someone has to accept before they'll buy. Each traces to a specific line in `offerbrief.md` or `avatar-sheet.md`.

1. **I believe that "quiet" is a number, not a marketing word.** → Traces to `offerbrief.md`'s core mechanism (published decibel measurement per switch) and `deepresearch.md`'s finding that the category's trusted comparison tool has zero decibel data. Without this belief, the published-dB angle is just noise to them.

2. **I believe I can type at my normal, full speed and still be quiet — I don't have to type softer or slower to keep the peace.** → Traces to the offer's promise line ("low acoustic output at full typing speed, not just at a light touch") in `offerbrief.md`.

3. **I believe a pre-lubed switch can be just as quiet as one I'd lube myself, so I'm not giving anything up by skipping the DIY step.** → Traces to `offerbrief.md`'s unique mechanism (pre-lubed, no DIY lubing step) and the objection in `avatar-sheet.md` ("silent switches feel mushy / worse to type on").

4. **I believe the switch and the desk mat are one system, not two separate purchases — the mat is sized and dampened to match the set it ships with.** → Traces to `offerbrief.md`'s product description (switch set + pre-cut, sized-and-dampened desk mat, shipped together).

5. **I believe getting a new, measured-quiet setup every quarter is worth paying for on an ongoing basis, not a one-time fix I only need to buy once.** → Traces to the subscription/quarterly cadence in `offerbrief.md`'s product description.

6. **I believe the person I share walls with will actually notice the difference.** → Traces to `avatar-sheet.md`'s core pain (keyboard noise already named and complained about by the people around them) and desire (wants to stop being the person named in that complaint).
