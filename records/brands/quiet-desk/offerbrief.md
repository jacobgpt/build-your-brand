# Quiet Desk — Offer Brief

Grounded in: `deepresearch.md` (competitive contrast) and the user's own description of their product (self-reported facts about their own mechanism, not external claims requiring proof).

## Product

A quarterly subscription that ships two things together each cycle: a fresh mechanical keyboard switch set, and a pre-cut desk mat sized and dampened to match. (Source: user input.)

## Promise

Type at full, normal speed without the noise being a problem for whoever shares your walls — roommate, partner, or coworker. (Source: user input.)

## Unique mechanism

Two things, stacked:
1. **Selection and lubrication is optimized for measured acoustic output first**, not feel or price — every switch in the lineup is chosen because it tested quiet at full typing speed, not because it's popular, cheap, or has a satisfying bottom-out feel. (Source: user input.)
2. **Every switch ships pre-lubed with a published decibel measurement**, so there's no DIY lubing step and no unverified "quiet" marketing claim to take on faith. (Source: user input.)

Per `deepresearch.md`, this is a real point of difference: the category's most-used sound-comparison resource (Click and Thock) is built entirely on subjective audio clips with zero decibel data, and general keyboard-parts shops (NovelKeys, Divinikey, KBDfans) sell pre-lubed switches and desk mats as separate catalog items organized by feel/price/build-compatibility, not as a measured, bundled, recurring acoustic product.

## Proof

- Published decibel measurement for every switch shipped, each quarter. (Source: user input — this is the product's own stated proof mechanism, not an external claim requiring third-party verification.)
- Pre-lubed at the factory/fulfillment stage, removing the DIY lubing step entirely. (Source: user input.)
- Desk mat is pre-cut and sized/dampened specifically for the switch set it ships alongside, not a generic add-on. (Source: user input.)
