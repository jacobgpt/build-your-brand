# Signal Roasters — Brand Book

*Grounded in: `signal-roasters-research.md` (⚠️ test fixture, not live
research — see that file's header).*

## Positioning

Signal Roasters is coffee delivery timed to your actual consumption, not a
calendar date — for remote workers who treat their first cup as the
start-of-shift signal, not a treat. The obvious alternative (subscription
roasters on a fixed biweekly ship date) runs you out early or drowns you in
stale backlog; Signal ships to when you'll actually need it.

## Audience

Someone who works from a home office, has run out of coffee mid-morning on
a workday at least once, and finds that more annoying than a missed
package. They want the cup to be a dependable start-of-shift trigger, not a
ritual to savor.

## Tone

Plain, operational, a little dry — reads like equipment documentation, not
a love letter to coffee. Confident without adjectives doing the work.

**Never sounds like:** cozy, artisanal-with-a-story, or apologetic about
being a subscription.

## Visual pillars

1. Product photographed like equipment on a plain surface — flat lighting,
   no lifestyle staging, no hands-wrapped-around-mug shots
2. Palette stays desaturated and utilitarian (charcoal, bone, one signal
   accent color) — no warm golden-hour coffee-shop tones
3. Typography is a monospace or grotesk workhorse face, never a script or
   serif associated with "craft" branding
4. Every image includes a legible timing/schedule element (a date, a
   countdown, a delivery window) somewhere in frame — the mechanism is
   always visible, not implied
5. No beach, blanket, hygge, or seasonal-mood-board imagery under any
   circumstance

## What we refuse (the competitive fence)

- Lifestyle photography (blankets, sweaters, window light, someone gazing
  out a window with a mug)
- Seasonal gimmick flavors or copy referencing them (pumpkin spice, holiday
  blends)
- Merch upsells at checkout (mugs, tote bags, apparel) as a growth lever
- Any claim of "ethically sourced" or "small-batch craft" without a
  specific, checkable fact behind it
