THE GAP

Every subscription in the category ships on a fixed interval.

Weekly. Biweekly. Monthly. Pick a cadence and the service holds you to it — until it's wrong. The fix is left to you: notice the mismatch, then skip, delay, or swap a delivery to correct for it.

"Too much coffee on hand or running out before the next package arrived."

— a recurring complaint in subscription-coffee reviews

The schedule doesn't adjust. You do.

THE MECHANISM

Consumption-matched timing, not a calendar date.

Signal tracks how fast you actually go through a bag and roasts small batches to ship within a window built around when you'll need them — before you run out, not on a fixed day regardless of whether you need it.

Fixed-interval subscription
  • Ships on the same date every cycle
  • You skip or delay when timing is wrong
  • Sells variety, curation, unboxing
Signal Roasters
  • Ships to match your actual pace
  • Timing is the default, not a workaround
  • Sells reliability — nothing else
